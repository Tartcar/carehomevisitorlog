Privacy Policy — Care Home Visitor Log

Last updated: September 2026

Care Home Visitor Log is a visitor sign-in app for residential care homes. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Data stays on your iPad

Everything you enter in the app — visitor names, visit times, resident names, phone numbers, and your settings — is stored only on the iPad where the app is installed. The app has no accounts, no servers, and no internet connection. We do not receive, see, or store any of your information.

Nothing is collected or tracked

The app does not collect analytics, does not use advertising or tracking identifiers, and does not share any information with third parties.

Exports go only where you send them

You can export visit records as CSV or PDF files. Those files go only where you choose to send or save them (for example, emailing them to yourself or saving them to the Files app).

Backups and the admin PIN

Your iPad's own backups (iCloud or computer backups) may include the app's data — that is handled by Apple, not by us. The admin PIN is stored in the iPad's secure Keychain and does not leave the device; if you restore a backup onto a different iPad, you will need to set a new admin PIN.

Children

The app is designed for adult caregivers and visitors. It is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address and included in the app under Settings → About.
