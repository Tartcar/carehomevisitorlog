Privacy Policy — Care Home Visitor Log

Last updated: September 2026

Care Home Visitor Log helps care facilities manage visitor records. Your information is stored locally on the iPad. The app does not automatically send visitor records to the developer or operate a cloud-sync service. Device backups and exports are explained below.

Information stored in the app

The app stores information entered by facility staff and visitors, including:

  • Visitor names, the resident or purpose of the visit, and the visitor's relationship or role
  • Sign-in and sign-out times
  • Optional phone numbers and notes
  • Health-screening self-attestations, when enabled
  • Resident names, the facility name, and app settings

The admin PIN is protected using the iPad's Keychain. The app stores a protected representation of the PIN rather than the PIN itself.

How information is used

Information is used to record visits, show who is currently signed in, maintain visit history, and create reports requested by facility staff. Optional phone numbers may be used by the facility for visitor follow-up or contact tracing.

Facility staff manage the information entered into the app. Names displayed on visitor-facing screens may be visible to other people using the iPad.

No accounts, advertising, or tracking

The app does not require an online account and does not include advertising, analytics, or tracking tools. Visitor records are not automatically transmitted to the developer.

If you contact support, the developer receives the information you choose to include in your message. Please avoid sending resident information or visitor records unless necessary to resolve your request.

Exports and sharing

Authorized staff can export records as CSV or PDF files and choose where to save or share them.

Once exported, those files are separate from the app's records. Their storage and protection depend on the destination you choose, such as email, another app, or cloud storage.

Device backups

App data may be included in iCloud or computer backups, depending on the iPad's backup settings. These backups are managed outside the app.

The admin PIN is tied to the original device and does not transfer to another iPad through a backup. Restoring app data to another device requires setting up admin access again.

Retention and deletion

The app retains visit records until facility staff delete them. Staff can delete individual visits or use Settings → Data to clear visit records or reset the app.

Deleting information from the app does not remove previously exported files or copies retained in device backups. Those copies must be managed separately.

Visitors who have questions about their records or want to request a correction or deletion should contact the care facility where they signed in. The developer cannot access or change records stored on a facility's iPad.

Children's privacy

Care Home Visitor Log is intended for use by care facilities and their visitors. It is not designed for or marketed to children.
